How It Works

1

Citizen Submits Alert

Users fill out a simple form specifying emergency type, description, and location coordinates.

2

Intelligent Routing

The Dispatcher analyzes the alert and routes it to the appropriate service (Police, Fire, or Medical) via RPC.

3

Unit Dispatch

The service identifies the nearest available response unit, updates its status, and dispatches it to the location.

4

Confirmation & Tracking

The citizen receives confirmation, and administrators can track the alert status in real-time.
