How DEARS Works

Simple, fast, and efficient emergency response process

1

Citizen Submits Alert

Users fill out a simple form specifying emergency type, description, and location. GPS coordinates can be automatically captured for precise location.

2

Intelligent Routing

The Dispatcher analyzes the alert and routes it to the appropriate service (Police, Fire, or Medical) via RPC. Smart algorithms ensure optimal routing.

3

Unit Dispatch

The service identifies the nearest available response unit based on location and availability, updates its status, and dispatches it to your location.

4

Confirmation & Tracking

You receive immediate confirmation with a unique tracking ID. Administrators can monitor the alert status in real-time through the dashboard.
